# Risk Register

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Risk Register is to identify potential risks that may affect the successful implementation of the Master Data Governance project. It defines the likelihood, impact, mitigation strategy, ownership, and current status of each identified risk.

---

## 2. Risk Assessment Criteria

### Probability

| Rating | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Low | Unlikely to occur |
| Medium | May occur during the project |
| High | Likely to occur |

### Impact

| Rating | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Low | Minor effect on project objectives |
| Medium | Noticeable impact requiring corrective action |
| High | Significant impact on cost, schedule, quality, or business operations |

---

---

## 4. Risk Monitoring

Project risks shall be reviewed regularly during project meetings.

The following activities shall be performed:

- Review newly identified risks.
- Assess changes to probability or impact.
- Monitor mitigation effectiveness.
- Escalate critical risks.
- Close risks that are no longer applicable.

---

## 5. Risk Response Strategies

| Strategy | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Avoid | Eliminate the cause of the risk. |
| Mitigate | Reduce the probability or impact of the risk. |
| Transfer | Shift the responsibility to another party. |
| Accept | Monitor the risk without immediate action. |

---

## 6. Conclusion

Effective risk management supports successful project delivery by identifying potential issues early, assigning clear ownership, and implementing appropriate mitigation strategies. Maintaining this Risk Register throughout the project lifecycle improves decision making and increases the likelihood of achieving project objectives.
